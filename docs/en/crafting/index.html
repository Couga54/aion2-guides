Gathering and crafting

Which profession to pick, the fastest way to level it and how to get gold blanks for your crafted weapon.

Choose a profession

  • Gathering is open from level 1 on global. First decide what you want to gather and craft first.
  • Example for a Ranger: Handicrafting makes both bows and staves and accessories — the obvious pick. Choose the profession that crafts your main weapon.
  • Gathering and crafting both have two tiers — Novice → Professional — of 50 levels each.

Level up on ore and Odyle

  • The easiest way to level both gathering and crafting is ore. Start with Sapphire Gemstone in a high-level zone: 40–60 veins take you to level 50.
  • Then go back to the quest NPC in the main city and take the green quest: gather a small perfect ruby. Mine Ruby Gemstone until it procs — usually 2–3 veins; there are some in the pit near the city.
  • Crafting needs Odyle: it is gathered in the air, in every zone. A secluded zone where it spawns along a cliff is ideal — ride the air currents to save wing stamina.
  • Back in the city: recraft all sapphires, then craft rings. At level 50 take the next green quest from the city NPC — craft a quest item from starting materials. These quests also give the first Pantheon frames.
  • Time: about 40 minutes to Novice 50 (on Taiwan, with bots around); Odyle takes over an hour. Short on white Odyle — break green ones down in Substance Morph. Odyle also crafts Odyle Energy.

Wood, bows and gold blanks

  • Yggdrasil Log — a through-zone is best, and you can farm pets on the way. Be patient: many players pass there and the resource is worth more.
  • Gold level 60 blanks come from crafting blue bows with a crit — a 25% chance, pure luck. Crafting orbs drop everywhere: mobs in the world, the Abyss, dungeons. Rings work the same way.
  • For the whole leveling collect about 300 sapphire veins and about 140 trees. Got gold materials? Don't rush to sell them — you will need both Odyle and wood.
  • Sell only crit bows and rings (white and green); craft the non-crit ones into the next tier. Start crafting once you have the materials, level gathering while you kill mobs, and don't forget the gathering skill tab — for example, move the points to Odyle before gathering it.

Buy or morph

  • Every material for a gold weapon or ring can be bought on the market, and Substance Morph turns Odyle into any material. Don't rush: check the prices first — usually it's better to sell Odyle and buy two materials for one Odyle, but there have been times when it was the other way round.
  • Where each material spawns: aion2t.com resource map.
