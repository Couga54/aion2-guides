Overview

The Sorcerer is a ranged glass cannon that casts both fire and ice. Fire does the damage — Fire Mark marks the target and Blaze cashes it in — while ice slows, roots and freezes so nothing reaches you. Most of the burst sits in a 10-second window behind Element Enhancement.

The build in one sentence

Open with Delayed Explosion and Element Enhancement, drop Fire Wall and Cold Storm, then loop the Fire line and the Ice line with Flame Arrow keeping your MP above 50%.

Built from Korean guides (Vortex Gaming's post-balance PvE guide, MMO Codex) and translated to global skill names from questlog. There is no global Sorcerer build from a trusted player yet.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Sorcerer:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Korean builds run Element Enhancement, Fire Wall, Delayed Explosion and Cold Storm all at 20 — on global only one of them reaches 20 at first.
  • Skills at 16, not 20. Arcana and rings are what push skills past 16, and they come slowly — the picks below are chosen for 16.
  • No combat speed at the start. Long casts hurt; take the Skill Speed specializations where offered.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Macro & rotation

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

MB5Fire line
  1. 4 Flame Arrow
  2. 3 Firestorm
  3. 2 Blaze
  4. 1 Flame Scattershot

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Blaze only lights up on a Fire-Marked target, so it sits near the bottom; Flame Arrow fills the gaps and applies the next mark.

RMBIce line
  1. 3 Ice Chain
  2. 2 Frost
  3. 1 Frost Burst

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Frost Burst fires as soon as Frost lands; Ice Chain is the filler.

2. In-game macro

Bind it to the middle mouse button. Delay 10 ms on every slot (40–50 ms with high ping):

  1. 1Fire line10 ms
  2. 2Delayed Explosion10 ms
  3. 3Bittercold Wind10 ms
  4. 4Ice line10 ms

Hellfire is a charged skill and Element Enhancement is a buff for your burst window — keep both out of the macro.

3. Burst opener

  1. Delayed Explosion just before the tank pulls.
  2. Element Enhancement and Wish of Concentration.
  3. Fire Wall + Cold Storm under the boss.
  4. Bittercold Wind, then Winter's Shackles for the +20% damage boost.
  5. Hellfire charged to 3, then the macro.

4. What you actually press

  • HoldBasic attack on LMB, the Ice line on RMB, the macro on MMB.
  • By handThe burst opener every time Element Enhancement is back, Hellfire, Defiance.

Recap

  • Skills: Blaze, Flame Arrow, Bittercold Wind, Ice Chain, Winter's Shackles, Wish of Concentration 16; the rest 12.
  • Stigmas: Element Enhancement 20, Fire Wall 10, Cold Storm 10, Delayed Explosion 10.
  • Passives: Robe of Flame, Grace of Enhancement, Fire Mark, Robe of Earth.
  • Mana: keep it above 50% — Flame Arrow and Blaze restore MP.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Bittercold Wind (root + pull) → Hellfire → Ice Chain until they drop.
  • Elites: Flame Arrow for Fire Mark → Blaze → Firestorm, kiting with Ice Chain slows.
  • Mana: use target mode so the basic attack keeps running, and weave Flame Arrow.
  • Low HP: Steel Barrier and distance — the Sorcerer has no heal.
