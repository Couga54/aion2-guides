Overview

The Chanter fights in melee with a staff and makes the whole party stronger at the same time. Its mantras stay on permanently, Power of the Storm speeds up everyone's skills, and its own damage comes from a stun → Dark Crush combo that keeps looping.

The build in one sentence

Keep Undefeated Mantra and Sprint Mantra on, open with Power of the Storm, and spam the line where Impactful Crush and Spinning Strike unlock Dark Crush while Onslaught refills your MP.

This guide follows TitanTheF's global build (start 1–45 and the 45+ goal) from his AION 2 progression sheet.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Chanter:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Two of them are mantras, so there are only two free picks.
  • No combat speed at the start. Season 1 gear gives little attack speed, so the simple macro below is the best one for now — Power of the Storm matters even more.
  • Bigger groups (5-player expeditions, 10-player sanctuaries): every party buff reaches more people.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill. TitanTheF's sheet links a starting route for levels 1–45 — open it and take those nodes while leveling.

  • Priority: blue nodes for Onslaught, Dark Crush, Spinning Strike, then orange nodes.
  • Priority: blue nodes for Impactful Crush, Wave Blow, Defiance.
  • Ariel (PvE) at 45, not Azphel.
  • Azphel (PvP) at 45, and Triniel earlier.

Mantras are toggles: switch them on once and keep them out of any macro — a second press turns them off.

Macro & rotation

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

  1. 4 Incandescent Blow
  2. 3 Spinning Strike
  3. 2 Dark Crush
  4. 1 Impactful Crush

Impactful Crush and Spinning Strike unlock Dark Crush, which then fires as the next-highest priority; Incandescent Blow fills the gaps.

2. In-game macro

Bind it to the middle mouse button. Delay 10 ms on every slot:

  1. 1Power of the Storm10 ms
  2. 2Onslaught10 ms
  3. 3Wave line10 ms
  4. 4Onslaught10 ms

TitanTheF weaves Onslaught into the macro itself — it restores MP, and with no combat speed yet it's the simplest setup that works.

3. What you actually press

  • HoldOnslaught on LMB, the Crush line on RMB and the macro on MMB.
  • Mouse macroOr let your mouse software loop LMB → RMB → MMB (10 ms each) on one toggle button — TitanTheF uses Witchmana, but any mouse software works.
  • By handMantras (once), Recuperation when the party takes damage, the Dash line to close gaps, Defiance.

Recap

  • Skills: Onslaught, Dark Crush, Spinning Strike 20; Incandescent Blow, Impactful Crush, Defiance 16; the rest 12.
  • Stigmas: Undefeated Mantra 20, Power of the Storm 10, Sprint Mantra 10, Marchutan's Wrath 10.
  • Passives: Attack Preparation and Impact Hit 20, Wind's Promise 16, Blessing of Life and Earth's Promise 14.
  • Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy; guard to +10.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Rushing Smash in (resets on a kill) → Obliterate → Crush line + Onslaught.
  • Elites: Impactful Crush (stun) → Dark Crush → Wave Blow → Spinning Strike → Dark Crush again.
  • Mana: Onslaught restores MP on every hit — never stop weaving it.
  • Low HP: Recuperation, and Sprint Mantra heals on hits.

Leveling macro

TitanTheF's starting setup works from level 1: the Crush line on RMB, Onslaught on LMB and the in-game macro (Power of the Storm → Onslaught → Wave line → Onslaught) on the middle mouse button.
