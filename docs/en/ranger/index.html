Overview

The Ranger is a bow DPS that fights from about 20 metres — the range of almost every skill it has. Its damage is top-tier, but survivability and utility are low, so positioning is everything.

The build in one sentence

Stack buffs first — Gale Arrow, Marking Shot (Precision: +300 Critical Hit and +35% Deadshot damage), then the stigma buffs — and fire charged Deadshots while bleeds and burns tick.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). This guide follows the global build of TitanTheF, who played the EU test — not the Korean endgame. What that means for the Ranger:

  • Realistic skill levels. With the weaker global Daevanion and no Heroic gear, the goal is 16 on six core skills, not 20 on four. Arcana push the most important ones further.
  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. The global set is Supporting Fire, Vaizel's Authority, Bow of Blessing and Explosive Arrow — Griffon Arrow from Korean builds is out.
  • Gear: after the main story you'll be around 800 gear score. Which gear tier is best is still open until launch.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data, but Korean builds assume far more skill levels and stigma tiers than global Season 1 gives.

Passives

Focused Eye (16) carries the build, followed by Hunter's Resolve and Hunter's Soul (15). Everything else sits at 10.

  • Focused Eye — Accuracy, PvE Damage Boost and Double Chance; Vaizel's Authority multiplies it.
  • Hunter's Resolve — Critical Damage Boost.
  • Hunter's Soul — extra damage on crits.
  • Concentrated Fire and Rooting Eye — bonus hits on bleeding and on Slowed targets; 10 is enough.

Gear & stats

  • After the story: about 800 gear score; from there follow the 45+ progression (dungeons, crafting, Abyss).
  • Rings: +1 levels on your core skills.
  • Accuracy matters: a missed Deadshot is the biggest DPS loss a Ranger can have. Attack and Critical Hit next.
  • Which gear tier to chase is still open until the global launch.

Arcana

Look for cards that roll Snipe, Deadshot, Tempest Shot and Gale Arrow — those four gain the most from levels above 16.

A card leveled to 5 averages about +2 to a random skill it can roll, so expect to push two or three of them past 16 in Season 1. Deadshot and Gale Arrow first when you have to choose.

Rotation & macro

AION 2 has two separate tools, and the build uses both:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards below show each line top to bottom as in the game, with the priority number on every skill.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped. Hold your basic attack (LMB) together with the macro key instead of putting it into the macro — the game weaves and cancels it better that way.

Lines follow a Korean setup (7MMO) adapted to the global stigmas; TitanTheF will post his endgame macro after testing on live servers.

2. In-game macro

Skill window → Macro. Put the three lines in this order and set every Delay to 10 ms. Bind the macro in Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro (a side mouse button works best).

  1. 1Gale line1 · 10 ms
  2. 2Drill line2 · 10 ms
  3. 3Buff line3 · 10 ms

Never put Snipe or Mother Nature's Breath inside a line — they would fire instead of your stronger skills. Snipe stays on LMB and is held next to the macro.

Season 1 tip (Grobs): cooldowns are slow at launch, so buffs are worth more when timed — you can take the buff line out of the macro and press it yourself right before a boss or a big pack.

3. What you actually press

  • HoldSnipe (basic attack, shortens Deadshot's cooldown) and the Macro key.
  • Press-releaseDeadshot when it's up — a charged skill fires at a random charge stage inside a macro, so it stays on its own key.
  • Every ~15 sMarking Shot to refresh Precision.

Overview

A PvP Ranger doesn't win by trading blows — it wins by making sure no trade happens. Slows, roots, stuns, knockbacks and a silence keep enemies at 20 metres while Deadshot does the work.

Order of business: open from range or stealth → lock the target → burst under Precision → peel and reset distance.

No global PvP meta exists yet. This mode combines Korean/Taiwanese PvP advice with skill data from the global client — treat it as a starting point.

Combos & play

Opener

Stealth→Marking Shot→Suppressing Arrow→charged Deadshot

Against casters

Sealing Arrow→Snare Shot→Deadshot

Silence first, so they can't answer your burst with a shield or escape.

When a melee dives you

  • Ambush Kick or Gale Arrow knockback → Snare Shot → walk back to 20m.
  • Explosion Trap under your feet as they arrive.
  • Defiance for the first hard CC, Mother Nature's Breath when focused.

Recap

  • Skills: Deadshot 16→20; Marking Shot, Suppressing Arrow, Snare Shot, Defiance 16.
  • Stigmas: Ambush Kick, Sealing Arrow, Mother Nature's Breath + Stealth (Abyss) or Vaizel's Authority (Arena).
  • Passives: Focused Eye > Revitalization Contract / Unyielding Resolve.
  • Play: never trade in melee — lock, burst, reset distance.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Explosion Trap at your feet → Snare Shot → Drill Dart + Tempest Shot. They walk into the trap.
  • Elites: Marking Shot (Precision) → Snare Shot → Burst Arrow → charged Deadshot. Kite, don't tank.
  • Low HP: Drill Dart and Burst Arrow absorb HP. Step back out of melee range before you heal up.
