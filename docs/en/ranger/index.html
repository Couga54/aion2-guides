Overview

The Ranger is a bow DPS that fights from about 20 metres — the range of almost every skill it has. Its damage is top-tier, but survivability and utility are low, so positioning is everything.

The build in one sentence

Stack buffs first — Gale Arrow, Marking Shot (Precision: +300 Critical Hit and +35% Deadshot damage), then the stigma buffs — and fire charged Deadshots while bleeds and burns tick.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Ranger:

  • 4 stigma slots. Korean builds run five — the one that drops is the situational Explosive Arrow.
  • Stigmas cap at level 20. Bow of Blessing loses its +5s duration tier, and Supporting Fire its extra-shot tier.
  • No Heroic gear in Season 1: fewer bonus lines for skill levels and passives.
  • Weaker Daevanion bonuses — longer fights make buff uptime matter more.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data. Older Korean guides (before the stigma rework) put Snipe and Tempest Shot at 20 and skipped Gale Arrow — that advice is outdated.

Passives

Two passives carry the build: Focused Eye (Accuracy, PvE Damage Boost, Double Chance) and Hunter's Resolve (Critical Damage Boost). Push both as high as gear and cards allow.

  • Concentrated Fire — extra hits on targets taking damage over time (Drill Dart bleed, Griffon Arrow flames).
  • Hunter's Soul — extra damage on crits; Vaizel's Authority multiplies it.
  • Rooting Eye — extra damage on Slowed/Rooted targets; Snare Shot keeps it active.
  • Vigilant Eye and Unyielding Resolve are defensive — fine to leave where they land.

Gear & stats

  • Soulbind lines: Focused Eye, Hunter's Resolve and Attack on everything. On weapons: Combat Speed, Damage Boost, Weapon Damage Boost, Multi-Hit.
  • Rings: +1 levels on your four main skills (Deadshot, Gale Arrow, Drill Dart, Snare Shot).
  • Manastones: Front Attack Damage Boost first, then Weapon / Critical Damage Boost.
  • Accuracy matters: missed Deadshots are the biggest DPS loss a Ranger can have.

Korean endgame targets (Accuracy 2,600+, Critical Hit 3,150+, front damage boost 30–35%) come from a later gear tier than global Season 1 — use them as direction, not as a checklist.

Arcana

Goal: +4 on each of the four main skills — Deadshot, Gale Arrow, Drill Dart, Snare Shot. Leftover levels go into Focused Eye and Hunter's Resolve.

A card leveled to 5 averages about +2 to a random skill it can roll, so expect to finish at 18–20 on most of the four in Season 1. Prioritise Deadshot and Gale Arrow when you have to choose.

Rotation & macro

AION 2 has two separate tools, and the build uses both:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the first skill in the list that's off cooldown, so the order is the priority.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order, weaves basic attacks and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

Opener

Gale Arrow→Marking Shot→Vaizel's Authority + Bow of Blessing + Supporting Fire→Snare Shot→charged Deadshot

1. Hotbar lines

1Gale line
  1. 1 Gale Arrow
  2. 2 Griffon Arrow
  3. 3 Snare Shot
  4. 4 Explosive Arrow

Explosive Arrow only if you swap it into your stigmas.

2Drill line
  1. 1 Drill Dart
  2. 2 Burst Arrow
  3. 3 Tempest Shot

Burst Arrow only hits a Slowed or Rooted target — Snare Shot in line 1 sets it up.

3Buff line
  1. 1 Vaizel's Authority
  2. 2 Supporting Fire
  3. 3 Bow of Blessing

2. In-game macro

Skill window → Macro. Put the three lines, then Snipe, and set every Delay to 10 ms. Bind the macro in Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro (a side mouse button works best).

  1. 1Gale line1 · 10 ms
  2. 2Drill line2 · 10 ms
  3. 3Buff line3 · 10 ms
  4. 4Snipe10 ms

Never put Snipe or Mother Nature's Breath inside a line — they would fire instead of your stronger skills. Snipe goes in the macro as its own slot.

3. What you actually press

  • HoldSnipe (basic attack, shortens Deadshot's cooldown) and the Macro key.
  • Press-releaseDeadshot when it's up — a charged skill fires at a random charge stage inside a macro, so it stays on its own key.
  • Every ~15 sMarking Shot to refresh Precision.

Recap

  • Skills: Deadshot, Gale Arrow, Drill Dart, Snare Shot 20; Snipe, Tempest Shot, Arrow Scattershot 16; the rest 12.
  • Stigmas: Vaizel's Authority 20, Bow of Blessing 15–20, Griffon Arrow 20, Supporting Fire 5–15.
  • Passives: Focused Eye and Hunter's Resolve as high as possible.
  • Play: buffs first, fire Deadshot under Precision, never let Gale or Precision drop.

Overview

A PvP Ranger doesn't win by trading blows — it wins by making sure no trade happens. Slows, roots, stuns, knockbacks and a silence keep enemies at 20 metres while Deadshot does the work.

Order of business: open from range or stealth → lock the target → burst under Precision → peel and reset distance.

No global PvP meta exists yet. This mode combines Korean/Taiwanese PvP advice with skill data from the global client — treat it as a starting point.

Combos & play

Opener

Stealth→Marking Shot→Suppressing Arrow→charged Deadshot

Against casters

Sealing Arrow→Snare Shot→Deadshot

Silence first, so they can't answer your burst with a shield or escape.

When a melee dives you

  • Ambush Kick or Gale Arrow knockback → Snare Shot → walk back to 20m.
  • Explosion Trap under your feet as they arrive.
  • Defiance for the first hard CC, Mother Nature's Breath when focused.

Recap

  • Skills: Deadshot 16→20; Marking Shot, Suppressing Arrow, Snare Shot, Defiance 16.
  • Stigmas: Ambush Kick, Sealing Arrow, Mother Nature's Breath + Stealth (Abyss) or Vaizel's Authority (Arena).
  • Passives: Focused Eye > Revitalization Contract / Unyielding Resolve.
  • Play: never trade in melee — lock, burst, reset distance.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Explosion Trap at your feet → Snare Shot → Drill Dart + Tempest Shot. They walk into the trap.
  • Elites: Marking Shot (Precision) → Snare Shot → Burst Arrow → charged Deadshot. Kite, don't tank.
  • Low HP: Drill Dart and Burst Arrow absorb HP. Step back out of melee range before you heal up.
