Changelog

Everything that changed on the site, newest first.

Oct 6, 2026
  • New page: Dungeons — the bosses of Krao Cave, Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon, Vakron Sky Island, Fire Temple, Ferocious Horn Den, both Transcendence dungeons and Zikel in Nightmare: the mechanics that wipe a group, with arena diagrams and links to the moment in the video. In the Guides menu.
  • New page: Tier list (not final, subjective) — trueeevil, XifanGG, Kanon and KaidWOW side by side for PvE, mass PvP, solo PvP and pure DPS, with the average, plus how your ping changes the choice.
  • Suggest a change — every class guide now starts with a box that opens the feedback form; modes still built on Korean guides and classes without a PvP guide ask for a link to a global one.
  • Home page timers — next to “Global launch started”: the next Rift opening and the weekly reset.
  • World bosses by faction — tabs for Elyos, Asmodians and the Abyss; the page remembers yours. The site is now called AION 2 Global - Guides.
Oct 4, 2026
  • Turkish version — the whole site is now also in Turkish: guides, pages and trackers. As on the Ukrainian pages, skill and item names stay in English. The translation is made with AI — if you spot a mistake, use "Report a mistake" or open an issue on GitHub.
Oct 3, 2026
  • Gathering and crafting — the page is rewritten for global and made visual: the Essence Extraction skill tree (Proficient Handling and Delicate Touch to 10, Lady Luck at most 5, Odyle plus your weapon's resource), what each branch is for, Odyle morphing into any resource, and the crafting chain from grey to gold.
  • Elementalist: PvE and PvP — the class now has full PvE and PvP modes for Season 1, built on Evripides' global Spirit Master guide (aion2sm.com) and checked against DankRNG and aLuckyRO: skills and specializations, stigmas, passives, the buff-before-summon rule, rotation, macro, matchups and the Daevanion route.
  • World bosses — all 48 field bosses of Verteron and Altgard with their respawn timers: mark a kill and the page shows when the boss is back (and notifies you, if notifications are on). Every boss now shows where it spawns.
  • Sorcerer — skill priority rebuilt on a global level-45 Sorcerer (EUTOPIA): Hellfire and Firestorm are now core, Ice Chain is optional, Frost and Frost Burst leave the bar.
  • Chanter — from two Chanter mains on global (notXeon, WallyJTV): Recuperation 12 is enough, Power of the Storm is a core stigma, Inspiring Spell moves up, plus a macro tip for before Dark Crush 16.
  • Gladiator, Ranger, Templar, Assassin, Cleric — checked against the first global guides (aLuckyRO, Sen, LordRobson, trueeevil): day-one picks and alternatives added — Keen Strike and Aerial Snare on bosses, Explosive Arrow as an equal fourth stigma, Executing Blade for solo Templars, Determination over Impact Hit, early Cleric passives.
  • Leveling checklist — the stigma slots open one at a time at levels 22, 27, 32 and 37.
Oct 1, 2026
  • Ukrainian version — the whole site is now also in Ukrainian: guides, pages and trackers. Skill and item names stay in English there, since the game data has no Ukrainian. Switch languages with the button in the top bar.
Sep 30, 2026
  • New page: Game settings — what to change on day one, from Whelps' and Grobs' videos: control mode and targeting, the macro key (it has none by default), a separate key for Defiance, buff scrolls only in combat, boss orientation and aggro range, FPS and the options for mass PvP. In the Guides menu.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Sorcerer PvE rewritten — a setup for the first days at 45 from aLuckyRO's video: which specialization to take at skill level 8–10, four hotbar lines and one macro that keeps mana above 50%, target mode, Daevanion order. The guide is no longer marked as work in progress.
  • Where the builds come from — the Overview of every class guide now says it plainly: the guides are based on creators' videos from the Taiwanese servers (patch: TW), cut down to global Season 1. The “work in progress” marks are gone from all guides.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Assassin PvE rebuilt on Arthars Gaming's video — what to expect from the class in Season 1, new skill levels (Storm Rampage 16, Savage Roar only a filler), stigmas all in buffs (Illusive Clone, Swift Contract, Savage Fang, Triniel's Dagger), one damage line and the burst order.
  • Assassin: arcana and passives — a new arcana section (Illusion on the Mirror is a must) and passive priority for gear.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Specialization slots fixed — a skill has two slots from level 12 and the third only at 20. Gladiator, Templar, Sorcerer and Cleric cards no longer show three picks at level 16; each card says what the third slot takes at 20.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Chanter PvE rebuilt on Arthars Gaming's video guide — how Dark Crush opens, a new hotbar line and a two-step macro, skill levels and specializations, stigmas for four slots (Marchutan's Wrath 1, Focused Defense 5), passive priority.
  • Chanter: arcana — a new section: which stat, set and skills to look for on each of the five cards.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Cleric PvE: the macro in full — the three hotbar lines and the in-game macro steps read from Kaeria's video frame by frame, with the keys to hold and what to do on stagger. The guide is no longer marked as work in progress.
  • Cleric: Daevanion and arcana — the board route now skips the orange nodes that do nothing in PvE, set bonuses for arcana, Death as an extra stat, Benevolence as a healing swap, Voice of Doom corrected.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Daevanion boards in five more guides — Ranger, Assassin, Chanter, Sorcerer and Cleric now show the real global boards with the route to the nodes the guide takes and a tooltip on every node.
  • PvP guides in the same order as PvE — Gladiator and Templar PvP now start with the build at a glance, and the Daevanion boards sit between gear and stigmas.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Templar guide re-checked against SolAshur's video, frame by frame — Pummel specializations corrected, stigma sets for solo PvE and for dungeons, Assault Fury as a late PvP swap.
  • Templar: pets and gear — what to look for in each of the nine pet slots, item levels and attack of the weapons on the gear path, wing numbers, a note on PvP gear.
  • Templar: Daevanion boards — the real global boards with the route for PvE and PvP, and a tooltip on every node.
  • Daevanion in all guides — corrected for global: five boards, no Ariel; the level-45 board Azphel gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance.
Sep 30, 2026

  • Board tooltips — hover (or tap) any node on a Daevanion board to see the stat it gives and its cost, the same way as a skill.
  • Gladiator guide updated — boss opener, the boss orientation setting, Focused Block charges, Sword Aura Rampage, leveling priorities and arcana cards with tooltips.

  • First week: Shugo at 45, world bosses once each, alts advice. Sources: who to watch per class.

  • Item tooltips — hover an item on that page to see its description, item level, stats and arcana set bonuses.
