Changelog

Everything that changed on the site, newest first.

Sep 30, 2026
  • Sorcerer PvE rewritten — a setup for the first days at 45 from aLuckyRO's video: which specialization to take at skill level 8–10, four hotbar lines and one macro that keeps mana above 50%, target mode, Daevanion order. The guide is no longer marked as work in progress.
  • Where the builds come from — the Overview of every class guide now says it plainly: the guides are based on creators' videos from the Taiwanese servers (patch: TW), cut down to global Season 1. The “work in progress” marks are gone from all guides.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Assassin PvE rebuilt on Arthars Gaming's video — what to expect from the class in Season 1, new skill levels (Storm Rampage 16, Savage Roar only a filler), stigmas all in buffs (Illusive Clone, Swift Contract, Savage Fang, Triniel's Dagger), one damage line and the burst order.
  • Assassin: arcana and passives — a new arcana section (Illusion on the Mirror is a must) and passive priority for gear.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Specialization slots fixed — a skill has two slots from level 12 and the third only at 20. Gladiator, Templar, Sorcerer and Cleric cards no longer show three picks at level 16; each card says what the third slot takes at 20.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Chanter PvE rebuilt on Arthars Gaming's video guide — how Dark Crush opens, a new hotbar line and a two-step macro, skill levels and specializations, stigmas for four slots (Marchutan's Wrath 1, Focused Defense 5), passive priority.
  • Chanter: arcana — a new section: which stat, set and skills to look for on each of the five cards.
  • Contact — besides a GitHub issue, there is now a Contact on Discord button next to it and in the footer.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Cleric PvE: the macro in full — the three hotbar lines and the in-game macro steps read from Kaeria's video frame by frame, with the keys to hold and what to do on stagger. The guide is no longer marked as work in progress.
  • Cleric: Daevanion and arcana — the board route now skips the orange nodes that do nothing in PvE, set bonuses for arcana, Death as an extra stat, Benevolence as a healing swap, Voice of Doom corrected.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Daevanion boards in five more guides — Ranger, Assassin, Chanter, Sorcerer and Cleric now show the real global boards with the route to the nodes the guide takes and a tooltip on every node.
  • PvP guides in the same order as PvE — Gladiator and Templar PvP now start with the build at a glance, and the Daevanion boards sit between gear and stigmas.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Templar guide re-checked against SolAshur's video, frame by frame — Pummel specializations corrected, stigma sets for solo PvE and for dungeons, Assault Fury as a late PvP swap.
  • Templar: pets and gear — what to look for in each of the nine pet slots, item levels and attack of the weapons on the gear path, wing numbers, a note on PvP gear.
  • Templar: Daevanion boards — the real global boards with the route for PvE and PvP, and a tooltip on every node.
  • Daevanion in all guides — corrected for global: five boards, no Ariel; the level-45 board Azphel gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance.
Sep 30, 2026
  • Gladiator: Daevanion boards — the real global boards with the route for PvE, PvP and leveling (Grobs' 65-point route). Global has no Ariel board, only Azphel (PvP) — the guide is corrected.
  • Board tooltips — hover (or tap) any node on a Daevanion board to see the stat it gives and its cost, the same way as a skill.
  • Gladiator guide updated — boss opener, the boss orientation setting, Focused Block charges, Sword Aura Rampage, leveling priorities and arcana cards with tooltips.
Sep 30, 2026
  • New top menu — all pages are now in the Guides menu on the right; the class picker shows the full class name.
  • Who to watch on class pages — players of that class from the guide's sources and streamers, in your language.
Sep 29, 2026
  • After level 45: TitanTheF's route — Abyss farm, a crafted T1 weapon, the economic route and alts; plus how gear upgrades work (enhancement limits, manastones, Theostones, Soul Binding, titles, season).
  • New page: Gathering and crafting — which profession to pick, leveling on sapphires and Odyle, gold blanks and how much you need.
  • First week: Shugo at 45, world bosses once each, alts advice. Sources: who to watch per class.
Sep 29, 2026
  • New page: After level 45 — the item level route from 45 to the 2,800 dragon raid: what changes on global, Shugo, Nightmare, Draupnir, Vakron, Transcendence, a hardcore route and daily contract tips. Every block links to its moment in the video.
  • Item tooltips — hover an item on that page to see its description, item level, stats and arcana set bonuses.
Sep 29, 2026
  • After 45: the item level route — on the first-week page, from 45 to the 2,800 raid step by step (Shugo, Nightmare, Drupnir, Vakron, Transcendence). Based on the global datamine, so it is marked until confirmed.
  • First week updated — enemy-side rifts and feathers are no longer a priority on global; new tips on Shugo currency, Nightmare, daily contracts and pass boxes.
  • Notifications per boss — a bell next to each world boss turns its alerts on or off.
Sep 29, 2026
  • Templar rebuilt on SolAshur's global build — the Judgment / Punishment / Pummel trinity, stigmas per profile, macro, gear path, stats, pets, Pantheon and arcana. No longer WIP.
  • Cleric PvE (WIP) — from Kaeria's guide: a healer that deals damage, Earth Punishment + Condemnation, stigmas and arcana.
Sep 28, 2026
  • Cleric and Elementalist (WIP) — leveling guides with Grobs' early macros; PvE and PvP come once there is a real global source.
  • Search — press Ctrl+K (or /) to find a class, skill, boss or guide section.
  • Skill tooltips — hover any skill in a guide to see its description, cooldown and all specializations.
  • Notifications — the weekly tracker and the boss page can notify you when energy is full or a boss may be up (while the tab is open).
  • Sources with dates and error reports — every guide mode shows where its info comes from and when it was checked; "Report a mistake" opens a GitHub issue.
Sep 28, 2026
  • A livelier site — the home landscape drifts slowly with rising aether sparks; each class page has its own element particles, a breathing portrait and a glow in the class colour, and guide blocks fade in as you scroll.
Sep 28, 2026
  • World bosses — the global Season 1 field bosses (Verteron, Altgard, Lower Reshanta) with a kill tracker. Respawn timers come after launch.
  • Ranger PvE rebuilt on Whelps' guide — Deadshot and Gale Arrow to 20, Vaizel's Authority first, the two-line macro, soul binds, accuracy and arcana.
  • Only sourced guides — PvP for Ranger, Assassin, Chanter and Sorcerer is off until launch (no reliable source yet); modes built on Korean data carry a WIP note.
  • Smoother site — modes switch with a fade, the header folds smoothly, the class menu animates, class figures step out of their cards on hover, and the theme button shows a sun or a moon.
Sep 28, 2026
  • Sorcerer guide (WIP) — fire & ice build, stigmas, macro and leveling, assembled from Korean guides until proper global builds appear.
Sep 27, 2026
  • Chanter guide — TitanTheF's global build: skills, stigmas, mantras and the simple starting macro.
  • Assassin guide — Insignias, Heart Gore and the Illusive Clone burst window, with TitanTheF's global build.
  • Ranger reworked for global — realistic skill levels (16), the global stigma set and arcana targets instead of the Korean endgame.
  • Macros fixed — the bottom slot of a hotbar line has the highest priority; leveling macros for every class (Grobs).
Sep 27, 2026
  • Templar guide — PvE, PvP and leveling: skills, stigmas for global's 4 slots and a macro setup.
  • First week — what to finish before the first weekly reset and the one-time goals that are easy to miss.
