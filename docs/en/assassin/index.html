Overview

The Assassin is a dagger burst class. Savage Roar and other hits engrave Insignias on the target, Insignia Explosion cashes them in for damage and a stun, and every crit can fire Heart Gore. Most of the damage comes from the back, so positioning matters.

The build in one sentence

Stack Insignias with Savage Roar, pop Insignia Explosion on cooldown, spam Heart Gore on every crit — and in a burst window Illusive Clone removes Heart Gore's cooldown for 10s.

This guide follows TitanTheF's global build (start 1–45 and the 45+ goal) from his AION 2 progression sheet.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Assassin:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. The global set keeps the two burst stigmas at 20 and two utility picks at 10.
  • No combat speed at the start. Season 1 gear gives little attack speed, so a simple macro is the best one for now; Swift Contract is your speed.
  • Burst stigmas by hand. Cooldowns are long early on — Illusive Clone wasted on trash is a lot of lost boss damage.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

Macro & rotation

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

MMBInsignia line
  1. 4 Savage Roar
  2. 3 Ambush
  3. 2 Whirlwind Slice
  4. 1 Insignia Explosion

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Savage Roar builds Insignias; Insignia Explosion takes the top priority as soon as it's ready. This line is also the in-game macro.

1Stun line
  1. 3 Shadow Fall
  2. 2 Infiltrate
  3. 1 Shadowstrike

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Shadowstrike stuns, then Shadow Fall knocks the stunned target down.

2. In-game macro

Put the Insignia line into the macro (10 ms) and bind it to the middle mouse button. That's all — TitanTheF keeps it this simple because there's no combat speed at the start.

  1. 1Insignia lineMMB · 10 ms

3. What you actually press

  • HoldQuick Slice on LMB and the macro on MMB.
  • SpamHeart Gore on Q — it only fires on crits, so press it constantly.
  • Mouse macroOr let your mouse software loop LMB → MMB → Q (10–30 ms) on one toggle button — TitanTheF uses Witchmana, but any mouse software works.
  • By handIllusive Clone + Swift Contract on the boss, Evasion Stance for big hits, the Stun line, Throw Shadowblade on 2 while farming.

Recap

  • Skills: Savage Roar, Heart Gore, Insignia Explosion 20; Quick Slice, Ambush, Defiance 16; the rest 12.
  • Stigmas: Swift Contract 20, Illusive Clone 20, Evasion Stance 10, Throw Shadowblade 10.
  • Passives: Rear Smite 20, Assault Stance 18, Defense Break 15, Determination 14.
  • Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Savage Fang (5 Insignias) → Insignia Explosion → Whirlwind Slice; Throw Shadowblade resets on a kill for the next pull.
  • Elites: get behind → Ambush → Savage Roar to stack Insignias → Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore on crits.
  • Mana: Quick Slice restores MP — keep it on LMB.
  • Low HP: Shadow Fall absorbs HP; Defiance heals when you're stuck.

Leveling macro
