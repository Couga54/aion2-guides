Overview

Templar is AION 2's main tank and the class hard raids are built around. It holds aggro, soaks tank busters and — through Fury — turns every block into a damage buff for the whole party. It still deals respectable damage: Korean parties expect a geared Templar to do about 20–23% of the group's damage.

The build in one sentence

Shield skills trigger Judgment, so you press Shield Smite, Warding Strike and Doom Shield to keep Judgment firing, fill the gaps with Vicious Strike + Pummel and block the big hits to keep Fury on the party.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30), separate from the Korean/Taiwanese servers. What that means for Templar:

  • 4 stigma slots instead of 6 — Korean tank sets run five or six, so something has to go (see Stigmas).
  • Stigmas cap at level 20 — no level-25 tier in the global client.
  • No Heroic gear in Season 1: fewer bonus lines for Block and passives.
  • Weaker Daevanion bonuses: bosses live longer, so keeping Fury and Battlefield Banner up matters more.
  • 5-player expeditions and 10-player sanctuaries: Fury and Warding Strike's party tolerance hit more people.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data. Only stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

Gear & stats

  • Block — every block is Fury for the party and a Debilitating Smash proc. Don't drop it for pure damage.
  • Accuracy — a missed skill is lost aggro. Keep it high alongside defensive stats.
  • Attack %, damage amplification, critical damage on the remaining lines: Korean builds put Attack % on shoulders and cloak, damage amplification on the chest, combat speed on gloves.
  • HP and Defense matter more on the global servers early on, while the healer's gear is also weak.

Arcana: push Judgment to 20 first, then Vicious Strike, then Pummel and Punishment. Damage amplification, critical damage amplification, weapon damage and Attack are the stats to look for on unique cards.

Macro & rotation

AION 2 has two separate tools, and the build uses both:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards below show each line top to bottom as in the game, with the priority number on every skill.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped. Hold your basic attack (LMB) together with the macro key instead of putting it into the macro — the game weaves and cancels it better that way.

Templar's damage is a loop: shield skill → Judgment → procs → filler. The lines below are built so that Judgment and the procs always win when they light up.

1. Hotbar lines

RMBProc line
  1. 5 Pummel
  2. 4 Doom Shield
  3. 3 Debilitating Smash
  4. 2 Annihilate
  5. 1 Judgment

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Judgment, Annihilate and Debilitating Smash can only be used after their trigger, so the line falls through to Pummel the rest of the time.

ETrigger line
  1. 2 Warding Strike
  2. 1 Shield Smite

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Both trigger Judgment — together with Doom Shield they keep it firing.

RBuff line
  1. 2 Noble Armor
  2. 1 Battlefield Banner

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Banner on cooldown; add Noble Armor if you slot it.

2. In-game macro

Skill window → Macro. Put the three lines in this order and set every Delay to 10 ms. Bind the macro in Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro — a side mouse button works best.

  1. 1Proc lineRMB · 10 ms
  2. 2Trigger lineE · 10 ms
  3. 3Buff lineR · 10 ms

Season 1 tip (Grobs): cooldowns are slow at launch, so buffs are worth more when timed — you can take the buff line out of the macro and press it yourself right before a boss or a big pack.

3. What you actually press

  • HoldVicious Strike on LMB and the Macro key — Vicious Strike keeps your MP up between skills.
  • ChargePunishment to stage 3 by hand at the start of every window — charged skills fire at random stages inside a macro.
  • By handTaunt and Poach (aggro and pulls), Shield of Protection before tank busters, Shield Rush after a dodge.

Tanking is not the macro's job

Face the boss away from the party, block the telegraphed hits (every block is Fury for everyone) and keep Taunt's Shrink on the target. The macro only keeps your damage loop tight while you do that.

The lines follow Korean Templar setups (Judgment / Banner / Doom Shield / Pummel on one key, Vicious Strike held on LMB) adapted to global's four stigmas. Test them on the training dummy once the global client is live.

Overview

In PvP, Templar is a peeler and disruptor. It pulls targets out of position, stuns and knocks them down, cuts their healing and survives in the middle of a crowd as long as nobody gets behind it.

Priorities in order: control → staying alive → damage on whoever you just locked down.

No global PvP meta exists yet. This mode combines Korean PvP builds with skill data from the global client — treat it as a starting point.

Gear & arcana

Korean PvP Templars pick one of three directions:

  • Block-heavy — the current meta: survives several attackers as long as you don't expose your back. Weak against high-Accuracy builds.
  • Accuracy-heavy — answers evasive and blocking opponents; weak until you hit a stat threshold.
  • Damage — highest ceiling and best party contribution, but expensive and harder to control.

In Season 1 keep your PvE gear, stack Defense-type stats on everything except the weapon, and change stigmas, passives and Daevanion.

Combos & play

Pick and punish

Poach→Shield Smite→Judgment→Annihilate→charged Punishment

Engage

dodge + Shield Rush→Doom Shield→Debilitating Smash on the healer's target

Staying alive

  • Keep your back to a wall — block works only from the front.
  • Shield of Protection goes up for their burst, not after it.
  • Defiance for the first hard CC; Survival Willpower covers the next one.

  • Stigmas: Empyrean Lord's Punishment, Shield of Protection, Doom Shield, Noble Armor (Comrade in Arms for party fights).
  • Daevanion: Azphel over Ariel, Triniel earlier.
  • Play: pull, stun, anti-heal; block their burst and never show your back.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Poach one in (a kill resets it) → Warding Strike → hold Vicious Strike + the Pummel macro.
  • Elites: charged Punishment → Shield Smite (stun) → Judgment → Annihilate.
  • Mana: Vicious Strike restores MP and Pummel spends it — alternate them and you never run dry.
  • Low HP: Warding Strike heals and cuts damage; every block heals through Warding Shield.

Leveling macro

A simpler setup for leveling and the first days at the cap, from Grobs' early-game macros. Only the skills you spam go in; buffs, stigma bursts and gap-closers stay on their own keys.

1Line 1
  1. 4 Shield Smite
  2. 3 Judgment
  3. 2 Debilitating Smash
  4. 1 Doom Shield

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

2Line 2
  1. 2 Pummel
  2. 1 Warding Strike

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

In-game macro: Line 1 → Line 2, each with a 10 ms delay. Hold your basic attack Vicious Strike on LMB and the macro key together.

Once your skills reach 12–16 and specializations change their cooldowns, switch to the full setup in the PvE tab.
