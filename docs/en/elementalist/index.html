Overview

The Elementalist (Spiritmaster) is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements Elemental Fusion is ready. Dimensional Control fires each time a spirit uses its skill, and curses keep ticking on the target.

Leveling in one sentence

Level with Summon: Water Spirit (damage and mana), hold Cold Shock for MP, and let the in-game macro cycle the Spirit line and the Fusion line.

How to fight while leveling

  • Spirit: Summon: Water Spirit until the cap — best single-target damage, and it feeds your mana. The others stay at level 1 while leveling.
  • Packs: Jointstrike: Curse (hits up to 4) → Combustion → Elemental Fusion with the AoE pick once it's ready.
  • Mana: the main early problem — target mode, Cold Shock between skills, and end your spirit rotation on the Water Spirit.
  • Staggered targets: Rapid Scattershot.
  • Skip Soul's Cry — it's a PvP skill.

Leveling macro

Grobs' early-game setup: two hotbar lines and a two-slot in-game macro. In AION 2 the bottom slot of a line has the highest priority — the cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.

1Spirit line
  1. 4 Summon: Fire Spirit
  2. 3 Summon: Earth Spirit
  3. 2 Jointstrike: Corrode
  4. 1 Summon: Water Spirit

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Water Spirit first; Jointstrike: Corrode (a stigma) makes the target take more damage from your spirit.

2Fusion line
  1. 4 Combustion
  2. 3 Jointstrike: Curse
  3. 2 Dimensional Control
  4. 1 Elemental Fusion

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Elemental Fusion and Dimensional Control fire as soon as the spirits unlock them; Combustion fills the gaps.

In-game macro (Skill window → Macro), delay 10 ms on each slot:

  1. 1Spirit line1 · 10 ms
  2. 2Fusion line2 · 10 ms
  • HoldCold Shock on LMB and the macro key together.
  • By handEnhance: Spirit's Benediction, then Summon: Ancient Spirit (buff first — the Ancient Spirit lasts 30s).

Stigmas while leveling

Stigmas unlock at level 22.

  • Summon: Ancient Spirit Lv 5
    A 30-second extra spirit that also grants elements for Elemental Fusion.
    • 5+10% Ancient Spirit Attack
    All specializations
    • 5+10% Ancient Spirit Attack
    • 10x1.5 [Spirit's Descent] activation chance and damage for 30s
    • 15Ancient Spirit uses skill after 5 basic attacks
    • 20+20% Ancient Spirit Stats
  • Enhance: Spirit's Benediction Lv 5
    +20% PvE Damage Boost and Tolerance with healing for 10s — cast before the Ancient Spirit.
    • 5Restores 10% of Max HP on cast
    All specializations
    • 5Restores 10% of Max HP on cast
    • 10x1.5 [Spirit Strike] Damage Boost effect
    • 15x1.5 activation chance and amount healed from [Spirit Communion]
    • 20+25% PvE Damage Boost and Damage Tolerance, +12.5% PvP Damage Boost and Damage Tolerance for caster and Spirit
  • Jointstrike: Corrode Lv 5
    In Grobs' Spirit line: the target takes more damage from your spirit.
    • 5+15% extra damage taken from Spirit during Corrode
    All specializations
    • 5+15% extra damage taken from Spirit during Corrode
    • 10-20% Combat Speed on hit for 10s
    • 15-70% Incoming Heal during Corrode
    • 20+10s Corrode effect duration
  • Flame Blessing Lv 5
    Extra damage on hit for 10s — take it once your accuracy holds up.
    • 5+100 Accuracy
    All specializations
    • 5+100 Accuracy
    • 10+100 Critical Hit
    • 15+10% Critical Damage Boost
    • 20-50% extra damage cooldown
