After 45: the item level route

The order to gear up in until the first raid. Numbers are the item level you leave each step with.

Datamine From the global datamine a day before launch — not confirmed on live servers yet. Sanya Jacuzzi

  1. 45

    Story and your own map

    Finish the main story — it hands out a gold weapon, a bracelet and wings. Then the regional quests: 2 rings, 2 earrings, a necklace (blue) and 2 PvE runes. Enhance a little, set up your PvE macro and clear every ? on your side of the map; after that the remaining regional quests and the feathers.

  2. 1400

    Shugo, Nightmare, Drupnir

    Shugo Festa and boss rifts for Shugo currency, Nightmare for its currency — spend both on Daevanion nodes. Then Expedition: Conquest in Drupnir up to the guarantee (14 chest openings, 7 with premium): two gold items from drops, the third from the guarantee. One superior stone into the gold weapon and bracelet, intermediate stones into the Drupnir gold pieces, the cheapest stones into the blues, a green Theostone into the weapon. Still short? Three runs of Exploration: Drupnir guarantee another gold piece.

  3. 1900

    Vakron

    Farm Vakron Sky Island until you have almost the whole set — it is the only early source of a guard (or buy it for 2 tickets: one per 21st chest). Weapon and accessories are best crafted later. Take the set to +10. Still short? One run on each boss of Transcendence T1 — grey arcana add about 40.

  4. 2100

    Transcendence T2

    Run T2 until you get the two green slots — bell and mirror; fill the rest with grey arcana. Bonuses don't matter yet, only item level.

  5. 2500

    Next armor set

    Go straight to the next dungeon (“the Dog” in the RU community) and stay until you have most of its armor set; transfer your Vakron enhancement into it.

  6. 2800

    Gold arcana and the raid

    Farm gold arcana in Transcendence T4 and roll the skills you need for your PvE and PvP setups. At 2,800 the 10-player dragon raid opens — the hardest PvE of Season 1.
