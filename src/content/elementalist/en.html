The Elementalist (Spiritmaster) is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements {{ s('elemental-fusion') }} is ready. {{ s('dimensional-control') }} fires each time a spirit uses its skill, and curses keep ticking on the target.

Leveling in one sentence

Level with {{ s('summon-water-spirit') }} (damage and mana), hold {{ s('cold-shock') }} for MP, and let the in-game macro cycle the Spirit line and the Fusion line.

{% endcall %} {% call section('fight-lvl', t.lvl_combat, 'lvl') %}
  • Spirit: {{ s('summon-water-spirit') }} until the cap — best single-target damage, and it feeds your mana. The others stay at level 1 while leveling.
  • Packs: {{ s('jointstrike-curse') }} (hits up to 4) → {{ s('combustion') }} → {{ s('elemental-fusion') }} with the AoE pick once it's ready.
  • Mana: the main early problem — target mode, {{ s('cold-shock') }} between skills, and end your spirit rotation on the Water Spirit.
  • Staggered targets: {{ s('rapid-scattershot') }}.
  • Skip {{ s('souls-cry') }} — it's a PvP skill.

Grobs' early-game setup: two hotbar lines and a two-slot in-game macro. In AION 2 the bottom slot of a line has the highest priority — the cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
