The Ranger is a bow DPS that fights from about 20 metres — the range of almost every skill it has. Its damage is top-tier, but survivability and utility are low, so positioning is everything.

The build in one sentence

Stack buffs first — {{ s('gale-arrow') }}, {{ s('marking-shot') }} (Precision: +300 Critical Hit and +35% {{ s('deadshot', False) }} damage), then the stigma buffs — and fire charged {{ s('deadshot') }}s while bleeds and burns tick.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). This guide follows the global build of TitanTheF, who played the EU test — not the Korean endgame. What that means for the Ranger:

  • Realistic skill levels. With the weaker global Daevanion and no Heroic gear, the goal is 16 on six core skills, not 20 on four. Arcana push the most important ones further.
  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. The global set is Supporting Fire, Vaizel's Authority, Bow of Blessing and Explosive Arrow — Griffon Arrow from Korean builds is out.
  • Gear: after the main story you'll be around 800 gear score. Which gear tier is best is still open until launch.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data, but Korean builds assume far more skill levels and stigma tiers than global Season 1 gives.

{% endcall %} {% call section('macro', 'Rotation & macro', 'pve') %}

AION 2 has two separate tools, and the build uses both:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards below show each line top to bottom as in the game, with the priority number on every skill.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped. Hold your basic attack (LMB) together with the macro key instead of putting it into the macro — the game weaves and cancels it better that way.

Lines follow a Korean setup (7MMO) adapted to the global stigmas; TitanTheF will post his endgame macro after testing on live servers.

2. In-game macro

Skill window → Macro. Put the three lines in this order and set every Delay to 10 ms. Bind the macro in Settings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro (a side mouse button works best).

{{ macroseq([('Gale line', '1 · 10 ms'), ('Drill line', '2 · 10 ms'), ('Buff line', '3 · 10 ms')]) }}

Never put {{ s('snipe') }} or {{ s('mother-natures-breath') }} inside a line — they would fire instead of your stronger skills. Snipe stays on LMB and is held next to the macro.

Season 1 tip (Grobs): cooldowns are slow at launch, so buffs are worth more when timed — you can take the buff line out of the macro and press it yourself right before a boss or a big pack.

3. What you actually press

  • Hold{{ s('snipe') }} (basic attack, shortens Deadshot's cooldown) and the Macro key.
  • Press-release{{ s('deadshot') }} when it's up — a charged skill fires at a random charge stage inside a macro, so it stays on its own key.
  • Every ~15 s{{ s('marking-shot') }} to refresh Precision.

A PvP Ranger doesn't win by trading blows — it wins by making sure no trade happens. Slows, roots, stuns, knockbacks and a silence keep enemies at 20 metres while Deadshot does the work.

Order of business: open from range or stealth → lock the target → burst under Precision → peel and reset distance.

No global PvP meta exists yet. This mode combines Korean/Taiwanese PvP advice with skill data from the global client — treat it as a starting point.
