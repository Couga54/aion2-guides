The Chanter fights in melee with a staff and makes the whole party stronger at the same time. Its mantras stay on permanently, {{ s('power-of-the-storm') }} speeds up everyone's skills, and its own damage comes from a stun → {{ s('dark-crush') }} combo that keeps looping.

The build in one sentence

Keep {{ s('undefeated-mantra') }} and {{ s('sprint-mantra') }} on, open with {{ s('power-of-the-storm') }}, and spam the line where {{ s('impactful-crush') }} and {{ s('spinning-strike') }} unlock {{ s('dark-crush') }} while {{ s('onslaught') }} refills your MP.

This guide follows TitanTheF's global build (start 1–45 and the 45+ goal) from his AION 2 progression sheet.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Chanter:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Two of them are mantras, so there are only two free picks.
  • No combat speed at the start. Season 1 gear gives little attack speed, so the simple macro below is the best one for now — Power of the Storm matters even more.
  • Bigger groups (5-player expeditions, 10-player sanctuaries): every party buff reaches more people.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

Mantras are toggles: switch them on once and keep them out of any macro — a second press turns them off.

{% endcall %} {% call section('macro', 'Macro & rotation', 'pve') %}

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

{% call hotline('RMB', 'Crush line', ['impactful-crush', 'dark-crush', 'spinning-strike', 'incandescent-blow']) %}Impactful Crush and Spinning Strike unlock Dark Crush, which then fires as the next-highest priority; Incandescent Blow fills the gaps.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('2', 'Wave line', ['wave-blow', 'heat-wave-blow']) %}Wave Blow knocks down a stunned target.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('3', 'Dash line', ['rushing-smash', 'tremor-crush']) %}Gap-closers — press by hand.{% endcall %}

3. What you actually press

  • Hold{{ s('onslaught') }} on LMB, the Crush line on RMB and the macro on MMB.
  • Mouse macroOr let your mouse software loop LMB → RMB → MMB (10 ms each) on one toggle button — TitanTheF uses Witchmana, but any mouse software works.
  • By handMantras (once), {{ s('recuperation') }} when the party takes damage, the Dash line to close gaps, {{ s('defiance') }}.
