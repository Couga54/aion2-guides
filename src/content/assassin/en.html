The Assassin is a dagger burst class. {{ s('savage-roar') }} and other hits engrave Insignias on the target, {{ s('insignia-explosion') }} cashes them in for damage and a stun, and every crit can fire {{ s('heart-gore') }}. Most of the damage comes from the back, so positioning matters.

The build in one sentence

Stack Insignias with {{ s('savage-roar') }}, pop {{ s('insignia-explosion') }} on cooldown, spam {{ s('heart-gore') }} on every crit — and in a burst window {{ s('illusive-clone') }} removes Heart Gore's cooldown for 10s.

This guide follows TitanTheF's global build (start 1–45 and the 45+ goal) from his AION 2 progression sheet.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Assassin:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. The global set keeps the two burst stigmas at 20 and two utility picks at 10.
  • No combat speed at the start. Season 1 gear gives little attack speed, so a simple macro is the best one for now; {{ s('swift-contract') }} is your speed.
  • Burst stigmas by hand. Cooldowns are long early on — Illusive Clone wasted on trash is a lot of lost boss damage.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

{% endcall %} {% call section('macro', 'Macro & rotation', 'pve') %}

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

{% call hotline('MMB', 'Insignia line', ['insignia-explosion', 'whirlwind-slice', 'ambush', 'savage-roar']) %}Savage Roar builds Insignias; Insignia Explosion takes the top priority as soon as it's ready. This line is also the in-game macro.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('1', 'Stun line', ['shadowstrike', 'infiltrate', 'shadow-fall']) %}Shadowstrike stuns, then Shadow Fall knocks the stunned target down.{% endcall %}

2. In-game macro

Put the Insignia line into the macro (10 ms) and bind it to the middle mouse button. That's all — TitanTheF keeps it this simple because there's no combat speed at the start.

{{ macroseq([('Insignia line', 'MMB · 10 ms')]) }}

3. What you actually press

  • Hold{{ s('quick-slice') }} on LMB and the macro on MMB.
  • Spam{{ s('heart-gore') }} on Q — it only fires on crits, so press it constantly.
  • Mouse macroOr let your mouse software loop LMB → MMB → Q (10–30 ms) on one toggle button — TitanTheF uses Witchmana, but any mouse software works.
  • By hand{{ s('illusive-clone') }} + {{ s('swift-contract') }} on the boss, {{ s('evasion-stance') }} for big hits, the Stun line, {{ s('throw-shadowblade') }} on 2 while farming.
