The Sorcerer is a ranged glass cannon that casts both fire and ice. Fire does the damage — {{ s('fire-mark') }} marks the target and {{ s('blaze') }} cashes it in — while ice slows, roots and freezes so nothing reaches you. Most of the burst sits in a 10-second window behind {{ s('element-enhancement') }}.

The build in one sentence

Open with {{ s('delayed-explosion') }} and {{ s('element-enhancement') }}, drop {{ s('fire-wall') }} and {{ s('cold-storm') }}, then loop the Fire line and the Ice line with {{ s('flame-arrow') }} keeping your MP above 50%.

Built from Korean guides (Vortex Gaming's post-balance PvE guide, MMO Codex) and translated to global skill names from questlog. There is no global Sorcerer build from a trusted player yet.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Sorcerer:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Korean builds run Element Enhancement, Fire Wall, Delayed Explosion and Cold Storm all at 20 — on global only one of them reaches 20 at first.
  • Skills at 16, not 20. Arcana and rings are what push skills past 16, and they come slowly — the picks below are chosen for 16.
  • No combat speed at the start. Long casts hurt; take the Skill Speed specializations where offered.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

{% call hotline('MB5', 'Fire line', ['flame-scattershot', 'blaze', 'firestorm', 'flame-arrow']) %}Blaze only lights up on a Fire-Marked target, so it sits near the bottom; Flame Arrow fills the gaps and applies the next mark.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('RMB', 'Ice line', ['frost-burst', 'frost', 'ice-chain']) %}Frost Burst fires as soon as Frost lands; Ice Chain is the filler.{% endcall %}

2. In-game macro

Bind it to the middle mouse button. Delay 10 ms on every slot (40–50 ms with high ping):

{{ macroseq([('Fire line', '10 ms'), (skill_data('delayed-explosion')[lang].name, '10 ms'), (skill_data('bittercold-wind')[lang].name, '10 ms'), ('Ice line', '10 ms')]) }}

Hellfire is a charged skill and Element Enhancement is a buff for your burst window — keep both out of the macro.

3. Burst opener

  1. {{ s('delayed-explosion') }} just before the tank pulls.
  2. {{ s('element-enhancement') }} and {{ s('wish-of-concentration') }}.
  3. {{ s('fire-wall') }} + {{ s('cold-storm') }} under the boss.
  4. {{ s('bittercold-wind') }}, then {{ s('winters-shackles') }} for the +20% damage boost.
  5. {{ s('hellfire') }} charged to 3, then the macro.

4. What you actually press

  • HoldBasic attack on LMB, the Ice line on RMB, the macro on MMB.
  • By handThe burst opener every time {{ s('element-enhancement') }} is back, {{ s('hellfire') }}, {{ s('defiance') }}.
